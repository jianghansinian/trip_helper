Leaf Moments - China Tourism News

📅December 13, 2025
🌐Translated: English
📄View Original

"Red flowers and green leaves" is a familiar aesthetic image. "Safflower also needs green leaf support" is a widely spread aesthetic principle. In ancient poetry, whether it is the setting of "the new green between the branches is heavy, and the small buds are deeply hiding a few reds" or the backlash of "the infinite green leaves of the Tianlian lotus, and the sun lotus flowers are differently red", this aesthetic concept is constantly emphasized. Red flowers, like famous singers dressed in the center of the stage.The green leaves, as if they were accompanied by singers and dancers around the singing star. Has that ever been the case? Seems unlikely.The flowers may not be red, and the leaves may not be green.And different varieties of plants, even the same variety of plants in different seasons, will also be different. Has it always been so convenient and beautiful? It seems unlikely.I remember many years ago, at the Beijing National Culture Palace Grand Theatre, I saw a dance performance of Yang Liping's peak moment.It is not a dance accompaniment song, but a song accompaniment dance.What music was matched and what stage was installed at that time has been forgotten. Only her beautiful peacock dance has lingered in front of her for a long time, which is unforgettable.Of course, it can also be interpreted here as dancers being flowers and others being leaves. Four o'clock grass and trees came out in turn. This is the hue change you see when you live in Beijing for many years.At the end of the autumn and the beginning of the winter, the color was not flowers, but leaves. On the weekend afternoon, go for a walk in Dongfeng Park.The ground is covered with large leaves of sycamore trees, some of which are yellow in green, some of which are red in yellow, and some of which are transparent in red.A giant pentagonal leaf can cover a person's entire face.The infinite fallen leaves made the footsteps slippery and could not bear to move forward.My wife crouched on the ground and concentrated on picking. The surrounding leaves were colorful, like a person in the middle of a drawing board.
