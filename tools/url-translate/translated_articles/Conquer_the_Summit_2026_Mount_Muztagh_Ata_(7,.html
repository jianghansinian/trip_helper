**Conquer the "Father of Ice Mountains": Your Ultimate Guide to Mount Muztag Ata (7546m)**

**Introduction to the Route**
Mount Muztag Ata, standing at 7,546 meters, is more than a peak—it's a legend. Located on the border of Akto and Taxkorgan counties in Xinjiang, on the western edge of the Tarim Basin, it forms one of the three great peaks of the Eastern Pamirs alongside Kongur Tagh and Kongur Tiube.

Perpetually snow-capped and glittering with ice, its majestic form resembles a white-haired elder. Locals reverently call it "Muztag Ata," meaning "Father of Ice Mountains" in Kyrgyz, a title earned as one of the region's earliest glacial formations. This colossal mountain, a sacred beacon on the Pamir Plateau, is a dream destination for climbers worldwide.

The well-established West Ridge route offers relatively gentle slopes, making Muztag Ata an ideal first 7,000-meter peak and a perfect training ground for those aspiring to conquer 8,000-meter giants like Everest.

**Why Choose Muztag Ata?**
* **Ideal First 7,000m Peak:** Rounded summit, stunning scenery, fully developed glaciers, and lower technical demands offer an incredible climbing experience.
* **Gateway to 8,000m Peaks:** The premier training ground for Everest and other extreme altitudes.
* **Optimal Conditions:** The most accessible 7,000m peak with mature logistics, convenient travel, and 90% mobile signal coverage on the route.
* **Immersive Xinjiang Experience:** A deep dive into the region's unique culture, geography, nature, and cuisine.
* **National Recognition:** Successful female summiteers can apply for National First-Class Athlete honors; male summiteers for National Second-Class Athlete honors (processed by our team).

**Detailed Itinerary & Route Notes**
Our primary route is the mature and gradual West Ridge.
* **Base Camp (BC):** 4,450m. A 30-degree scree slope. Start slow to conserve energy for the ascent to C1.
* **Advance Base Camp (ABC):** 4,900m at the snowline. Transition point from animal to human porterage.
* **Camp 1 (C1):** 5,300m (3-4 hrs from BC). 80% scree, 20% snow/ice. The 5,700m-5,900m section is a crevasse zone—move with caution.
* **Camp 2 (C2):** 6,200m (5-7 hrs from C1). Full snow/ice terrain requiring crampons/snowshoes.
* **Camp 3 (C3):** 6,700m (5-8 hrs from C2). Steep snow slopes; navigate a crevasse field at ~6,410m.
* **Summit (7,546m):** 6-7 hrs from C3. A long, gradual snow walk. Mark your route carefully. The summit is marked by stone cairns. Limit your stay for a safe daylight descent.
